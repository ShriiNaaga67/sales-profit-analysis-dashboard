**Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview**

An interactive business dashboard developed using Google Sheets to analyze sales performance, costs, profitability, customer demographics, and sales trends.

**Business Objectives**
1.Track key sales, cost, and profit metrics.
2.Compare sales performance across countries and cities.
3.Analyze customer gender distribution by sales manager.
4.Compare current-year and previous-year sales trends.
5.Evaluate cost distribution across brands.
**Tools & Skills**
1.Google Sheets
2.Data visualization
3.Interactive dashboard filters
4.KPI reporting
5.Business performance analysis
**Dashboard Features**
1.Sales, Cost, and Profit KPI cards
2.Country-wise sales performance
3.Customer gender distribution by sales manager
4.City-wise sales and profit comparison
5.Current-year vs. previous-year sales trend
6.Cost distribution by brand
7.Filters for Country, City, Sales Manager, Month, and Brand
**Key Metrics Displayed**
1.Total Sales: 1,458,000
2.Total Cost: 1,174,000
3.Reported Profit: 499,000

**Project Purpose**

This project demonstrates spreadsheet-based dashboard development, data visualization, KPI reporting, and business performance analysis for portfolio purposes.
